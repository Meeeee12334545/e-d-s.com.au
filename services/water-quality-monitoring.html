Water Quality Monitoring

pH, EC, turbidity and nutrient monitoring for wastewater, process water and the environment, from one sensor to a whole network.

We’ve measured water quality for the wastewater industry since we began, and it’s still a big part of what we do. From one EC point to thousands of instruments reporting in real time, we supply, install and look after the equipment.

We make our own pH, EC and turbidity sensors and the iLab multi-parameter range, and we represent Aquamonitrix for real-time nitrate and nitrite. Readings go straight to FlowSense, or to your own SCADA or telemetry system.

What we measure

  • pH, EC and turbidityEDS-made sensors for continuous measurement in water and wastewater.
  • Several parameters at onceThe iLab 901 measures up to seven at once, from a list including pH, ORP, conductivity, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, ammonia and nitrate. The handheld iLab Sonde covers field checks.
  • Nitrate and nitriteAquamonitrix measures both from one sample, with no pre-treatment. Detection limits are 1.5 ppm nitrate and 0.25 ppm nitrite.
  • Flow and quality togetherOne program can cover both, as EDS has done for the wastewater industry for decades.

Where it is used

  • Wastewater networks and treatment processes
  • Raw, process and effluent water
  • Trade waste discharges
  • Rivers, stormwater and surface water
  • Baseline, impact and compliance monitoring for projects

Built for Long Deployments

Readings where you need themContinuous readings to SCADA, telemetry or a building management system, as well as to EDS FlowSense.
Few site visitsAquamonitrix runs more than 600 samples between services: over three months at six a day. The iLab 901 cleans itself.
Sensors that stay clear in a sewerSensor Fort, designed and built by EDS, holds pH, EC and other sensors in the manhole chase of a gravity sewer with only their tips in the flow, so rag has nothing to hold.
Up and running quicklyAquamonitrix can be running within a couple of hours, and its calibration stays stable over long deployments.
Calibrated in placeWe audit and calibrate analytical equipment on site, so the readings stay trustworthy.

Pump stations are where EDS began. We launched in 1991 with our flagship product, the Pump Station Manager (PSM), and we still design and build the instruments that watch over pump stations.

We supply and install monitoring for wet well levels, pump runs and alarms, and connect it to your SCADA. Flow data from upstream adds what SCADA can’t show: how the catchment responds to rain, and whether pump start and stop levels suit the flows arriving.

Utilities have used flow data from LIDoTT Smart sensors to refine those levels, cutting run time and power use.

What we provide

Pump Station ManagerThe EDS flagship since 1991, designed and built by EDS.
Wet well levelRadar and pressure level sensing with the LIDoTT R, or EDS ultrasonic level sensors.
Loggers and telemetryEDS EMS data loggers and the 4G Metalog send readings in real time.
SCADA integrationWorks with ClearSCADA, Schneider CITECT, ELPRO and other SCADA systems.

Applications

  • Wet well level and overflow alarms
  • Refining pump start and stop levels
  • Reducing pump run time and power consumption
  • Cross-checking network flow data against pump station records

Pump Station Manager in FlowSense

FlowSense works out station flow and pump condition from the level and run signals a station already has. No flow meter needed.

  • What it needs

    The wet well level signal, the run state of each pump, and the dimensions of the well. Most stations already produce the first two.

  • What it reports

    Volume in and out, pump starts and run hours, and energy per megalitre pumped.

  • Capacity against nameplate

    Each pump’s delivered capacity, set against its nameplate rating, shows wear before it becomes a failure.

  • Honest about gaps

    Cycles the record can’t support are left out and reported, not estimated.

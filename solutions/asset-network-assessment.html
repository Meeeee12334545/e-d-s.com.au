A deteriorating network rarely fails all at once. Long before anything fails outright, it starts costing more to run, in staff time and maintenance.

Regular assessments from EDS catch that slide early and keep your network performing at its best.

Accurate flow data shows far more than inflow. At one regional council, a sudden shift between depth and velocity revealed a partial blockage in a rising main, and CCTV confirmed the debris.

What we look for

Blockages and sedimentA steady rise in depth with no rain to explain it, found and cleared before the next storm.
Structural defectsDepth anomalies that point to sags, displaced joints and infiltration points.
Cross connectionsOvernight flow in dry weather, or a rainfall response a residential catchment shouldn’t have.
Where to spendInflow severity mapped across catchments, so inspections and rehabilitation go where they’re needed.

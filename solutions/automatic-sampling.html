EDS has the widest range of automatic water and wastewater samplers in Australia, and supplies the only true intrinsically safe sampler in the country.

Buy the setup your site needs, or hire one for a short study or a long program.

Suitable for

  • Water, stormwater and surface water sampling
  • River and bore sampling
  • Catchment monitoring
  • Process water monitoring
  • Product sampling for quality verification
  • Wastewater and pretreatment sludge sampling
  • Monitoring of sewer systems

Choosing the right sampler

Hazardous areaThe site’s hazardous area classification, and the ATEX and IECEx approval it needs.
Sample typeSpot, timed or composite samples, and how many bottles.
Time or flowTime-proportional or flow-proportional sampling.
RefrigerationWhether samples must be refrigerated until collection.
PowerBattery or mains power, and how long the sampler must run unattended.
TelemetryWhether readings and alarms need to be sent by telemetry.

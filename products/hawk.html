Hawk

A 4G logger that lives inside the manhole, with no cabinet to build and no power to bring to site.

Up to 152 AhInternal battery and a 40 µA sleep current

The Hawk goes inside the manhole. It powers and reads your flow, level and quality sensors over RS485, and logs as often as every second. It stores every reading on board and reports over 4G LTE to FlowSense and your own systems.

There’s no cabinet to build, no power to bring to site, and no need to enter the chamber to set it up. Nothing sits at the surface, so there’s nothing to damage, vandalise or maintain in the road reserve.

Hawk connects directly to E-Flow 3 and any other Modbus RTU sensor. One team looks after the sensor, logger, telemetry and data.

Sealed for surchargeCanister, lid and connectors are sealed to IP68, so the record continues while the chamber is underwater.
Built for the sewer atmosphereA V0 flame-retardant, corrosion-resistant ABS canister, sealed connectors and a stainless wall bracket.
No power at site72 to 152 Ah on board, and each sensor is powered only while it is read. No mains, no solar panel, no roadside cabinet.
Set up from the surfaceA magnet wakes the Hawk and EMS-Flow sets it up over Bluetooth from the road. Configuration and firmware follow over 4G.
Every reading kept32 MB on board holds 10 years of data, so a missed report in patchy coverage doesn’t mean lost data.
More than one destinationUp to four data centres. Send the same data to FlowSense and to your SCADA or historian, or run them as primary and standby.

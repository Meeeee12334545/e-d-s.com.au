Alarm2

All-in-one radar level monitor with alarms, up to 20 m, LoRaWAN or 4G.

Alarm2 is a complete level monitor and alarm for remote sites. Radar sensor, battery and modem share one compact enclosure, with an external antenna. It measures up to 20 m with no deadband, accurate to ± 5 mm. An alarm goes out the moment a threshold is crossed and hourly while it lasts, and a daily heartbeat confirms it’s working.

Precise across the whole rangeRadar measures from 0 to 20 m with no deadband, and stays accurate to ± 5 mm even at 20 m.
Flexible alarm configurationHigh and low set-points, rate of change and profile alarms are all set during installation. Alarms go out immediately, then hourly while the condition lasts.
Robust deploymentSealed to IP68 and built for manholes and chambers. Choose the LoRaWAN or 4G version to suit the network at each site.
Real-time monitoringData goes to your monitoring platform at each send interval, and straight away when a level changes state. A daily heartbeat confirms the unit is working, and a JSON API links it to cloud platforms and SCADA.
Low maintenanceNo external sensors or wiring. Battery life of over five years means fewer site visits.

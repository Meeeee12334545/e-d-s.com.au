    Australian owned. Family founded. Still measuring.

    1991

    EDS is founded

    Graham and Cynthia Harper established EDS in Queensland, with the release of its flagship product, the Pump Station Manager (PSM).

    2003

    Defence facility contract

    EDS began servicing and monitoring the Australian Defence facility, under a contract still in place.

    2023

    Sydney Water panel provider

    EDS was awarded a five-year panel contract to supply sewer flow and level instruments to Sydney Water.

    2026

    Transurban service provider

    EDS was awarded a service provider contract with Transurban to service and maintain key tunnel infrastructure.

    EDS is now Australia’s largest supplier of equipment for the water and wastewater industry. It was also awarded the largest sewer monitoring project in Australia in recent years.
